Technical · core decision

Few-shot anomaly scoring with explainable residual surfaces

For proxy anomaly triage, frozen DINOv2 features are compared to a normal support subspace. High residual regions become heatmaps, scores, and inspection decisions. This gives engineers a visible reason to review an image, not just a label.

1. Encode the inspection tile z = frozen_encoder(tile)
2. Fit normal reference subspace N = PCA(normal_support)
3. Score what does not fit normal residual = || z - project_N(z) ||
4. Turn evidence into an operator decision score -> calibration -> PASS / REVIEW / HOLD
